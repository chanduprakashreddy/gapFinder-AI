# Biology 101: Cells and Energy. Syllabus

Total: 100 marks. The marks for each unit are shown in brackets.

Unit 1. Cell Structure and Function (15 marks)
Prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells, organelles and their functions, the plasma membrane, diffusion, osmosis and active transport.

Unit 2. Photosynthesis (20 marks)
The chloroplast, light-dependent reactions, the Calvin cycle, limiting factors.

Unit 3. Cellular Respiration (20 marks)
Glycolysis, the link reaction, the Krebs cycle, oxidative phosphorylation, anaerobic respiration and fermentation.

Unit 4. DNA and Replication (20 marks)
Structure of DNA, semi-conservative replication, leading and lagging strands.

Unit 5. Mendelian Genetics (25 marks)
Alleles, Mendel's laws, monohybrid and dihybrid crosses, test crosses, codominance and sex linkage.
